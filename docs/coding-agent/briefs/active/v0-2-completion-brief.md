## 4a. Amendment: the v0.2 design (ratified 2026-10-16)

This amendment carries the outcome of the re-examination section 4 waited for. Owner, 2026-10-16, asked whether to keep the fixed room with reserved seats per cue kind or to return to the philosophy's picture: "Revise the way things are recalled, to fit with how we defined the philosophy to be." It traces to philosophy 7.2 to 7.6 and 8.1. An engineering view from a GPT-family model was obtained and is kept verbatim in the discussion notes; where it raised a doubt the statement below is marked provisional.

What a person experiences, as the design:

- [told, gives] **What comes back is a few situations, by weight, down to the bound the application gives.** Alice walks in during a conversation about the garden, with a promise to her overdue; what reaches the model is Alice (present, the promise attached, overdue by a week), the garden conversation, and the room's tone, whole, not twelve memories in reserved slots. No room is reserved per kind of cue, nothing is admitted to fill a slot, and the output has no fixed sections. [provisional] Any coverage preference the measurements show to be needed (so that a loud topic cannot crowd out the person present) is stated openly as a policy in the design, never hidden in weights or floors.
- [agent-proposed, gives] **Weight is made of ingredients that are comparable across kinds, and the result reports them.** How much it matters, how long since it was last touched, whether it is owed and to whom and whether it is due, whether its party is present, whether it was ever said, how it felt, how far it departed from expectation. A search's similarity score is how a topic found a situation and a condition of its relevance, never its weight. Missing ingredients are reported as unknown, never read as insignificance.
- [agent-proposed, gives] **A situation comes whole, within a bound, and says when it is not whole.** An unsettled matter gathers what rests on it through its own links and, through shared subject and thread, what bears on it that nobody linked; the result says by which path each part came, and where the bound cut the gathering it says so and offers to continue. [provisional] Where neither a shared anchor nor recognizable content exists, bounded retrieval cannot find the connection; that is consolidation's work in v0.3 and the result does not pretend otherwise.
- [agent-proposed, gives] **Familiarity decides what a thing brings, not how the cue was given.** A person or place the character knows well brings what is new or unresolved with them and the character's disposition as background; a person or place met once brings that one occasion; a description matching only by resemblance brings a reminder labelled as such. "New" is measured against the recorded experience, never against the last retrieval, so reading memory never consumes novelty. The history of a familiar thing is never hidden: it is reached whole the moment anything touches it. [agent-proposed, means] Familiarity is read from how large a part of the character's experience a thing has been, combined with how often and how recently; the share measure that exists today is a seed, not the whole of it.
- [told, gives] **Nothing is a real result, in three states, each with its scope.** Nothing found (the attempted paths returned nothing; coverage may be incomplete); something thin (a name that resolves to more than one notion or to a notion with little behind it, or a match connected only by wording); verified absence (an exhaustive, healthy structural check finds no record for a stated name, predicate or range at a stated snapshot). A miss by resemblance never shows that nothing happened. The states reach the character in the ordinary result, not only the trace.
- [agent-proposed, constraint] **Two guarantees, kept distinct.** Bounded foreground recall, which is what a turn receives; and complete structural access, by which every memory reachable by what it is (person, place, thread, time, identifier) can be reached in full through resumable retrieval that neither skips nor silently stops. Reach by resemblance is bounded, measured against a null distribution on the intended embedder and surface, and degrades visibly, with coverage reported.
- [inferred, means] The one bounded hop from a surfaced obligation (ADR-D-0023) stays and is how an obligation begins to gather its situation; weak associations do not open history; aboutness and presence stay distinct; scene cues are considered before the topic; the write path is untouched.
- [told, constraint] Time stays the clock in v0.2 (section 3); experience-counted time is not part of this amendment.

Records, each reaching the owner as the document itself: ADR-D-0022 (route per cue kind with a floor per route), ADR-D-0004 (the pack with typed sections) and ADR-I-0006 (per-section caps) are contradicted and are to be replaced by records the Orchestrator proposes and the owner accepts; ADR-I-0036 and ADR-D-0039, both proposed, are returned. ADR-D-0023 is strengthened; ADR-D-0009, -0018, -0024, -0027, -0029, -0033, -0034, -0037, -0038 and ADR-I-0010, -0024, -0026 are intact and constrain the design.

Measurement, [agent-proposed, constraint]: before anything learned or generative is added to selection, a deterministic situation view is compared with the existing behaviour on the existing pressure cases (the overlapping office and topic cases, two people with separate obligations, duplicated accounts, old high-salience matters, keyless scenes, empty queries), against topic-only and each-cue-removed controls; candidate recall, situation grouping, coverage, unrelated admissions and rendered behaviour are measured separately; cost is measured at years-sized stores with skewed hubs before any latency is promised.

What this leaves standing of the open stack: the storage and schema work, time's substance, the obligation, resolution, lifecycle, scene, write-turn and notion tests, the situated fixtures, the obligations and time families. What it replaces: the recall selection and room, the result contract, the renderer as specified, and the tests and calibration families that measured floors, standing and room.

Pass conditions: agent-checkable, the result carries situations with weights and ingredients and the three absence states, no reserved room and no fixed sections, and the measurement above is recorded; human-only, the owner's blind reading at closeout (section 3).
