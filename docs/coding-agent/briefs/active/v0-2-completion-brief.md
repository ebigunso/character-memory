## 2. The standard: the continuity situation catalog

[agent-proposed, gives] The catalog, `docs/design/continuity_situation_catalog.md`, is revised so that every statement of the ratified philosophy has a situation that exercises it and the philosophy's measure and ranked failures have a place in it. Owner, 2026-10-16, after the re-examination of each situation as a human and as an ideal AI character: "Yes, now it looks much better." The revision:

- adds a section of the failures that outweigh success: **invented continuity**, written as a guarantee on recall and on the character's inner state, not on its words (owner, 2026-10-16: "The thing that should be guaranteed is that recalled memory marks what it didn't remember as that, and the character's inner thought has reflected that. How that manifests as behavior depends on how that character tends to behave"); **lost continuity**, blankness toward something plainly lived, stated as a declared departure from human memory; **the mirror**, where accommodation is human and the failure is remembered belief acting as pressure on the character's own view; and the philosophy's month-long measure, stated as a judgement made by a reading and not a scenario;
- adds thirteen situations, each stating the human shape of what is in mind and how it is held, and where the ideal AI character departs: a familiar place (history not the default content, may surface involuntarily when the moment is quiet, comes back whole when touched); a place visited once; the constant companion (what is new or unresolved, plus held state about them, which is reflection's product); return after a long absence (the place's history is now in mind as memory; dispositions toward the people do not drift in silence, a declared departure, and reflection run during the absence counts as experience); lately (a heavy week colours every scene, as shaping, including scenes with unrelated people); the flinch before the reason (the disposition handed without its reason in the pack, the reason one hop away); something never lived (three states: nothing found, something thin, verified absence; what the character then says is its own, and a graceful bluff with an honest inner state is legitimate); the same situation, two characters (testable from v0.3); a story told five times (retellings are the character's own sayings, the tale does not grow); a full week and an empty one (the same event feels longer ago after the full week; an empty week is empty only for the AI, whose between-times thinking is reflection; it also knows the exact date); a tense talk that ended warmly (peak or end, both cases, the middle still there in full); the remark that became the last thing she said (gains weight, keeps the words); the hundred Tuesdays (the ordinary merge in what is in mind, each still findable, and what counts as odd depends on what the character attends to);
- amends the purpose paragraph so the standard is human behaviour with the philosophy's stated departures, said once rather than per situation;
- adds target variants for a topic, a group, a time of year and the self under existing situations, since every situation today keys on a person, place, thread or date;
- is written throughout as guarantees on what is in mind and how it is held, not as restrictions on behaviour. [agent-proposed, means] Owner, 2026-10-16, on the restriction-shaped version: "making that part of the acceptance criteria makes the criteria too strict."

Pass condition, agent-checkable: each philosophy statement named in the discussion notes' mapping has at least one situation; the failures section exists. Human-only: the owner accepts the revised catalog.

## 3. The roadmap

- [agent-proposed, gives] **v0.2 closes with a reading of behaviour.** Before v0.2 is judged complete, a small set of transcripts of a character carried through catalog situations with a model in the loop is read by the owner, under four conditions, each binding: the character's memory comes from material not written for the probes (the LoCoMo and LongMemEval conversations, ingested through the ordinary write path with no per-probe editing); the probes are chosen after recall and the renderer are frozen, by a Reviewer who did not build them; a control arm runs the same probes against the same model with no memory and against plain top-k over the same store; the owner reads blind to which arm produced each transcript. Negative probes (something never lived) are included, and v0.2's limit is stated in advance: the character has history but no self of its own, so the reading judges what recall hands over and how it is labelled. Owner, 2026-10-16: "If it's even possible to have the described check in place with an acceptable low level of bias, when every memory piece is being drafted for the test, then it's worth doing. But if because of the way we build the test we cannot tell if the data was biased or the test genuinely passed, then there's no point." On the four conditions: "I think those suffice." [inferred, constraint] If any of the four conditions cannot be met, the reading is not run and v0.2 closes on the retrieval tier with that limitation written into its closeout.
- [inferred, means] The renderer and the example loop are built ahead of the evals re-baseline and the paid benchmark runs, since the reading needs them and the paid runs should measure a design that has been seen to behave.
- [agent-proposed, gives] **v0.3's measure and definition of done.** v0.3 is measured by the two-characters reading against the strongest control, the same model with the whole transcript in context; what is sought is behaviour shaped by lived experience against generic behaviour with the same facts. Owner, 2026-10-16: "The part I feel differentiates the two is, how reflection shapes and persists the character's own thoughts and tendencies, which further reinforces how it behaves later. A character with such a memory would exhibit behavior that was shaped by those lived experiences, while a fresh session with the transcript would be more generic in how it behaves." Reflection's intent is stated in those terms. Its definition of done gains: a disposition toward a thing with felt tone, aggregated by reflection from the episodes' own accounts; the carried state and how things have been lately; surprise as what lasts; tendencies held with the situations they were seen in and their counterexamples; the character's own sayings as their own kind of evidence; compression that keeps peak and ending, cause and central people. Attribution's core, witnessed or told, moves into v0.3. Time counted in experience goes in v0.3 if it is cheap over the trace store, otherwise as the first named item of v0.4. Owner, 2026-10-16: "Great. Looks good now. I take your proposed suggestion for the open question as well."
- [agent-proposed, gives] **v0.4 and v0.5 are corrected where they contradict the philosophy or accepted records, at the altitude stated and no further.** v0.4 becomes what the character holds about the world over time: validity intervals, aliases and roles over time, who else knows, source reliability as the character's own belief about a source; the fact-acceptance machinery (Assertion, Claim, EvidenceLink, BeliefAssessment, SourceAssessment with confidence and stance) is removed from the roadmap rather than kept conditional, because the question it answers is not this library's. v0.5 becomes the long horizon proper: a decade of gists, recognition of a returning stranger, query-time associative activation, durable units on measured demand; decay and reinforce leave its draft. Familiarity and gist consolidation move down to the phases that need them. Owner, 2026-10-16: "I think your suggestions are sound at the level you gave. Fleshing out future shapes has its limits, so we shall clear things up only to a point where findings from prior phase measurements wouldn't pose a significant uncertainty."

Pass condition, agent-checkable: the roadmap and the phase drafts carry these changes and no others at that altitude. Human-only: the owner accepts the revised roadmap sections.
