# Plan: the moment itself brings memories to mind: what just happened, the dates a question names, and this day in earlier years

- status: in-progress
- generated: 2026-09-21
- last_updated: 2026-09-22
- work_type: code

## Goal
- A character arrives at a moment already holding what it did lately, so in the evening it can say how its day went without anyone supplying a topic that happens to match. When a question names a span of time ("last Tuesday", "in March"), what happened then comes to mind even if its words share nothing with the question. On the day something happened in an earlier year, that experience comes back, and is sure of its place when the person it was shared with is present. And when the character recalls something it believes, it knows how long ago that belief was last supported by anything it lived through, so an old belief is held as old and never dropped for being old.

## Definition of Done
- Recency is a road that contributes a few experiences and promises which are contributed, not which survive. It works as a description does since the scene-words slice: every retrieval contributes the latest episodes at or before the scene's reference time, by recorded scene time, as many as the largest cap in force could hold, as explicit roots of the cue kind recency, so the room nothing else claims fills with what happened lately; the rest of what is recent is not a candidate. A contributed occasion enters with no similarity score of its own (a cue component of zero) and reminder-only standing; at root selection it receives exactly its floor reservation, takes no spare turn, and is admitted otherwise only into room left after every given kind's queue is exhausted, so at a floor of zero with the roots full it takes no root at all; everywhere else it competes in the existing score order, so a real match wins by itself and only a faint or a distant one (a low similarity, or a far hop with a small inherited share) can lose to it. With a scene and no topic, what it contributes and what rests on those occasions is what comes. Its floor ships at zero and the calibration may raise it; at zero it still contributes its single latest occasion.
- A retrieval can carry a time range beside the topic, as it carries the activity: two instants the application gives, echoed in the result. It is a given cue and never a filter: the most recent episodes whose recorded scene time lies inside it, as many as the larger of one and the date-match floor, are contributed as explicit roots of the cue kind date match, hold that floor at root selection and the section caps, take no spare turn at roots (no time kind does) and otherwise enter only room left after every given kind's queue is exhausted, the rest inside the range are not a candidate, and the trace says whether more existed than came, and everything any other cue brings is admitted exactly as before. The library parses no language and calls no model.
- The same calendar day in an earlier year brings its episodes as date match. The calendar day is the day where the experience happened, read from the offset the application gave. Such an occasion is contributed the same way, the most recent first; it holds the date-match floor only when it is shared with a notion the present scene resolves, and otherwise enters with no reserved room, as recency does.
- The own-day scenario passes in both forms a deployment produces (arriving with no topic, by recency; being asked about the day, by the range the application gives), and an episode from another day is shown not to come by the range.
- Time never changes a score. Pack order is the existing combined score everywhere; what this plan promises is which memories are contributed. A memory also found by another cue keeps exactly the score (cue and graph components alike), standing and position it would have without the time cue: being a time root raises nothing that a full-standing road reached. A memory reached only by a time cue is a reminder: it brings what rests on that occasion and does not open a notion's or a thread's history. A time kind names direct contributions only: the contributed occasions and what rests on them within the leaf rule; provenance inherited through an interpreted memory to another episode (an occasion, a memory derived from it, and an older episode that memory also rests on) does not carry the time kind, which is the leaf rule applied to provenance, so an episode outside the contributed set is never named recency or date match.
- Recency and date match join the cue kinds in the trace and the values in `context.cue_floors`, through the one existing helper. The phase draft's "temporal rationale category" is delivered as these two cue kinds: the rationale enumeration it named no longer exists, and cue kinds in the trace replaced it.
- Every admitted interpreted memory reports, in the result and with the trace off, the age in whole seconds between the scene's reference time and the latest experience that supports it. Nothing is removed, omitted or reordered because of it (ADR-D-0018).
- No ranking weight for elapsed time ships. Whether one helps is a measurement for the companion repository once these roads exist, and it becomes a default only through the replacement of ADR-I-0022 that carries its numbers.
- Retrieval reads no clock: the reference scene is captured once and bound into every read, and the same store, scene and input give the same result on any day.

## Planner-added requirements
- A floor guarantees room only for a cue the scene or the application gave. Needed because: the time alone is on every scene, so it is not a cue anyone gave, and a floor for it would take room on every retrieval merely by existing. Recency therefore ships with a floor of zero: it contributes its single latest occasion with no reserved room, and that occasion competes by score alone. A range is given by the application, so it holds a floor. Neither time kind takes a spare turn at root selection: a kind present takes spare turns today whatever its floor, so a zero-floor recency root would still take a root slot from a strong topic root; the helper's spare-turn rounds therefore skip the time kinds, which receive exactly their floor reservation and then only room left after every given kind's queue is exhausted. This is decision 39 made concrete at the only stage that uses turns.
- An anniversary holds reserved room only when it is shared with a notion the present scene resolves; otherwise it is contributed with no reserved room, like recency. The floor is served only from the reserved queue: a shared anniversary is contributed with the date-match kind and floor eligibility; an unshared one bears the kind on the root, for the trace and the result, but the helper's date-match reservation queue does not contain it, so it competes only for unclaimed room. Omitting it from the date-match order is not enough, since the helper queues every candidate bearing the kind and would pick it at a cap of one. Needed because: today's date is on every scene and is not a given cue, while the person present is; the two together are D9. This conditions reserved room and never recall: a person still remembers the day their father died with nobody in the room, whenever the room is not already full. Shared with is read through the existing both-direction Involves and Mentions-to-episode semantics, never by scanning the participant literal.
- Time never changes a score, and a time cue contributes only a few occasions. Needed because: the existing rank key holds no time, and the scene-words slice replaced turns outside root selection with score order among honest scores (rulings 44, 45 and 47). A time cue has no similarity score, so a time root cannot borrow one: it enters as an explicit root with a cue component of zero, no vector score and no full-standing score, which is the treatment a reminder-only root has today (its standing, its leaf rule at expansion, and floors served to full-standing members of its kind first), except that a description root carries its honest scene-surface cosine as its cue component and a time root carries none. Its final score is therefore the graph and salience components alone. Ruling 47 applies to proximity as it does to the cue: a time-only or reminder-only root contributes its root proximity only to itself and to what rests on it, and an object also reached by a full-standing road keeps the graph component of its full-standing roads and is not raised by being a time root (today the merge takes the larger graph component from any road, which would raise a participant-reached occasion from one half to one merely for being the latest). On a duplicate the merge keeps the larger cue score and the union of kinds, so a topic hit or a keyed occasion that a time cue also reaches keeps its cue and graph components, its full standing and its final score unchanged from the parent, and gains only the time kind. Crowd-out is prevented by construction, not by ordering: a time cue contributes at most as many as the largest cap in force could hold, and the rest is not a candidate at all.
- `Scene.time` carries the offset the application gave (`DateTime<FixedOffset>`), the stored instant stays as it is today, and commit derives the experience's local date from the offset given and stores it on the episode. Needed because: an anniversary is a calendar day, and a calendar day in UTC is a made-up day for any deployment away from UTC (08:00 at +09:00 is 23:00 the day before). Deriving it in Rust at commit keeps the anniversary read an exact match on the local month and day with a local year earlier than the present scene's local year, so no offset handling happens in the query engine and no scan is needed. Earlier by instant is not earlier by year across offsets (00:30 at +14:00 on a date is before the start of that date at +09:00 and is the same local year), so the year compared is the local one.
- The derived local date is part of what a write says. Needed because: equality of offset-carrying times ignores the offset, so without it a same-id write with the same instant and a different local day would pass as a replay while changing which anniversary recalls the experience.
- The range and the anniversary report one kind, date match. Needed because: the ruled vocabulary is closed and names recency and date match; a memory whose date lies in the span a question names and one whose date is today's in an earlier year both match a date. One kind, two sources.
- The promise is episodes and what rests on them. Needed because: a time root is expanded as any explicit root is, so a reminder brings what was observed and concluded on that occasion through the existing links, and no separate observation lookup is added; a notion or a thread reached from a time-only root is a leaf (ruling 46), so being reminded of a day does not open the history of everyone who was there, and the same leaf rule applies to provenance: an interpreted memory resting on the occasion is brought, but another episode that memory also rests on is not named by the time kind and is not opened through it. The baseline proves what expansion brings from an unlinked occasion and the README says what comes.
- The two new floor values join the calibration the cues plan's closing task already hands to the companion repository, and no calibration task is added here. Needed because: one calibration over all shipped kinds measures them against each other.

## Design
- Chosen, the road: the time route is three reads over recorded scene times that contribute explicit roots with a cue kind, selected through `select_with_cue_floors`, exactly as a description contributes its occasions since the scene-words slice. Structure: crate-internal selectors on the graph port return episode id, recorded time and retention without full-store hydration: the latest at or before an instant; those between two instants; those with a given local month and day and an earlier local year. Each source contributes only the larger of one and its kind's floor value: recency the latest by recorded scene time then id at or before the reference time; the range the most recent inside it then id; the anniversary the most recent matching day then id, those shared with a notion the present scene resolves before the rest. Eligibility (only episodes that may be recalled, ruling item 34) is applied before the cut, so an older match is never lost behind a prefix of non-matches, and the rest of each source's matches are not candidates at all: the trace reports one left-out count per time source, as it does per description search. A contributed occasion is an explicit root with a cue component of zero, no vector score and no full-standing score: reminder-only standing, the treatment the helper and expansion already give a root that no full-standing road reached (the standing and leaf rule of rulings 46 and 47), the difference being that a description root carries its honest scene-surface cosine and a time root carries no similarity at all. Its final score is the graph component (one at the root itself, when nothing else reached it) and its salience, with no time term. A time root that duplicates an existing root merges by the existing rule for the cue: the larger score, the union of kinds, full standing if any road gives it; and by ruling 47 for proximity: the reminder road's proximity is set only on objects no full-standing road reached, so a memory the topic or a known participant also reached keeps its cue and graph components and its final score exactly as at the parent, and expands as before. The contributed occasions head their kind's order for the floor at every cap; at root selection the time kinds are excluded from the spare-turn rounds, receiving exactly their floor reservation and then only room left after every given kind's queue is exhausted; at the candidate merge they are absent (it holds vector candidates only) and at the section caps, after each kind's floor is served from its own order, spare fill is the caller's score order. Evolution: due dates and triggers join the same helper in the prospective-memory plan. Verification: service-free on the embedded stores through the public facade; each task first shows its case failing at its parent commit. Operation: the first two reads examine episode time metadata and are linear in episodes (the known ceiling; a chronological index is the upgrade if measured slow); the anniversary read is an exact match. No model call, no clock. Human: the application passes the time it perceives with its offset and, when a question names a span, the span. Safety: nothing is withheld by time; every source only adds candidates.
- Alternative: recency as a ranking weight only. Rejected by ADR-D-0022: a weight cannot admit what was never a candidate, and a scene with no topic has no candidates.
- Alternative: recency with a floor on every retrieval (the first draft, which also kept it out of spare-room sharing). Rejected by ruling: a floor is for a cue someone gave; the time is on every scene, so that floor would move every topical result for no cue at all. A zero floor that still contributes one occasion says the same thing with no special case, and is the rule a description already follows.
- Alternative: time-only candidates considered after every candidate with a given kind at every cap (the approved draft). Withdrawn on 2026-09-22: the scene-words slice withdrew turns and group ordering outside root selection (rulings 45 and 47) and replaced crowd-out prevention with contributing few occasions and letting honest scores decide; the same helper now serves this plan, and a second ordering rule inside it for time kinds would reintroduce what was withdrawn.
- Chosen, the principle for reserved room: given cues hold floors (participant, place, activity, a range, an anniversary shared with someone present); ungiven ones (recency, an anniversary shared with nobody present) are named in the trace and hold no reserved room. Spare turns at root selection are for the given non-time kinds only: the helper's kind list becomes participant, place, activity, date match, topic, recency, and its spare-turn rounds skip date match and recency, which receive exactly their floor reservation and then only room left after every given kind's queue is exhausted. An unshared anniversary is carried in the smallest form consistent with the helper's existing inputs: the helper takes a kind set per candidate and the caller builds that input at each call site, so the caller passes such a root with date match removed from the set the helper sees (an owned filtered set for that root only), while the root and its ranked object keep date match in their own cue kinds for the trace and the result; the helper's reservation queue therefore never contains it, and it neither holds the floor nor spends the date-match turn. If the helper's borrowed input cannot take a filtered set without a change to its rule for the kinds it already serves, stop and report.
- Chosen, selection (which are contributed; never pack order): recency contributes the latest by recorded scene time, then id, as many as the largest cap in force could hold, and its floor (zero by default) reserves nothing of that room; the contributed set is newest first as a selection order only; the range and the anniversary likewise the most recent inside their match, the anniversary's shared ones first. Among what was contributed the existing score decides everywhere, so under a tight cap a contributed occasion of high salience may win over a faint match and loses to a real one, and one older than the contributed set never comes by that cue. An episode reached through several links is counted once before the cut.
- Alternative: order the pack most recent first for time candidates. Rejected by ruling: time never changes a score; the rank key stays score, type, id. Chronological presentation belongs to the renderer.
- Alternative: recent and salient chosen from a window. Rejected for now: the window is a number nobody measured.
- Chosen, the range input: a start and an end instant beside the topic, both required (an open span ends at the scene's time). An end before its start matches nothing and is echoed as given; no new error. ADR-D-0023 allows it because it says when the question is about, not what the character wants.
- Alternative: the library finds the dates in the topic. Rejected: it interprets language inside retrieval and makes recall wait on a model or a grammar per language (ADR-I-0013).
- Alternative: the range filters the content search. Rejected as the meaning of the input: time never gates recall. A content search restricted to the span is left until a scenario fails without it; the README states the ceiling.
- Chosen, the calendar day: `Scene.time` carries the offset; the stored instant literal is unchanged; commit derives the local date from the offset given and stores it on the episode; the anniversary read matches the present scene's local month and day exactly with a local year earlier than the present scene's local year. Whether the stored form is one literal or a month-day key beside a year is the worker's choice, provided the read is an exact match without full-store hydration. Alternative: earlier year judged by instant. Rejected: wrong across offsets. Alternative: compare in UTC. Rejected: wrong for every deployment away from UTC, silently. Alternative: store the offset-carrying literal and extract month and day in the query engine. Rejected: the engine leaves an offset beyond fourteen hours unbound and a filter then drops the row silently, the read is a scan, and chrono equality ignores the offset so replay could not tell two local days apart. Alternative: a UTC offset given only at retrieval. Rejected: wrong whenever the character moved or the clocks changed. Episodes written before this plan have no local date and never match an anniversary; there is no migration.
- Chosen, staleness: a computed fact on the admitted interpreted memory's existing memory-scenes entry. Alternative: leave it to the application. Rejected unless the baseline shows otherwise: the plan review found the result collapses an observation into its parent scene and discards the observation's own time, so a consumer cannot derive it in every case. Alternative: omit or demote memories past an age. Rejected by ADR-D-0018.
- Measurement (standing rule of 2026-09-22): every task that changes what comes to mind, its order or its standing carries a measurement validation owned by the evals side, run at the task's tip before its pull request opens, before and after on the same instrument and inputs, in both id orders, twice. Instrument: the companion evaluation repository's Task_9 generated runner extended with a time family: a store of dated experiences with ids opposed to time, a scene giving only a time, a range beside a topic, and an anniversary shared with a scene-resolved notion and one unshared. Falsifiers, any one of which falsifies the design: the occasion contributed by recency is not the latest by recorded scene time; at a recency floor of zero a topic keeps fewer on-topic memories with recency present than alone; date match holds reserved room for an unshared anniversary. Task_1 measures the first two, Task_2 the second with the range present, Task_3 the third; Task_4 reports an age and changes nothing that comes, so it is exempt. The validator's vocabulary has no measurement kind, so the item is carried as a manual validation owned by the orchestrator, whose detail names the evals worker as the runner.
- Why chosen: the smallest shape that makes "when" a cue like a description of where and with whom, contributing a few occasions and letting scores decide, without letting it take room from cues someone gave. Fit: the phase draft sections 2 and 2.1; philosophy "make time a first-class dimension" and "the character knows how things stand before it has reflected"; ADR-D-0018, D-0022, D-0023, D-0029, D-0038, ADR-I-0013, I-0022.
