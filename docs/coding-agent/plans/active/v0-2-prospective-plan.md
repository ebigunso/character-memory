# Plan: the character carries what it owes, what it is owed, and what fell due

- status: in-progress
- generated: 2026-09-22
- last_updated: 2026-09-22
- work_type: code

## Goal
- A character that meets someone arrives knowing what stands open between them in both directions: what it promised them and what they promised it. A promise that fell due today, or that is overdue, comes to mind on that day and every day after until it is settled, whatever the conversation is about. The character knows which side of each obligation it is on, because the application told it at construction which notion is itself, and nothing else about recall changes for knowing that.

## Definition of Done
- An open loop or a commitment can say who owes whom: its actors and its counterparts, each one of the memory's own notion subjects, given by the application through the closed assertion vocabulary (ADR-D-0035) and never inferred. Several actors or several counterparts are true obligations ("I promised Bob and Alice"). A role on a subject the memory is not about, both roles on one subject, or a role on a memory that is not an open loop or a commitment, is rejected at the write. The stored shape is the existing assertion node; no new field carries a party.
- The facade is told at construction which notion is the character itself (ADR-D-0020). The identity has one recall effect and one reporting effect. Recall: the self never triggers, because the character's own presence never arrives, and a listed self would otherwise bring every own obligation at the trigger floor on every scene (ruling 12); a person's own undated promises surface by topic, by place, by their due instant, or by the self given as a participant through the state route, and the README says so. Reporting: the direction on an admitted obligation is the self among its actors or among its counterparts. It is never a cue, never a root, never a floor, never a filter, never added to a scene, never required to exist in the store as a notion, and never changes a score.
- The identity is kept as one literal in the store's metadata, written on first open; opening the store with a different identity is refused with a plain error. This prevents a silently inverted direction: a store opened as someone else would report every I-owe-you as you-owe-me. The literal is not a notion and nothing in recall reads it.
- An open loop or a commitment can carry a due instant, given by the application as an instant and never parsed from text. A due instant on any other kind of interpreted memory is rejected at the write. The instant is part of what a write says, so a replay with another instant is a content difference.
- The trigger route: for every notion the present scene resolves other than the self, the unresolved open loops and commitments in which that notion is a party (its actor or its counterpart) are contributed as explicit roots of full standing with the ruled cue kind trigger, in the notion's own scope, so several people present each bring their obligations before any brings a second. A party is a key or a resolved name; a description is never an identity and triggers nothing. The route reads through the state route's selector and its resolution filter: a resolved or fulfilled obligation is left out with the existing resolution reason, and stays reachable by every other road, marked resolved as today.
- The due route: the unresolved open loops and commitments whose due instant falls on or before the scene's local day (the day the scene's offset gives, as the time plan derives it) are contributed as explicit roots of full standing with the ruled cue kind due, whatever the topic. Overdue obligations keep coming until resolved. Nothing later than the scene's local day is contributed by this road.
- Trigger and due hold floors as ADR-D-0022's stored intention's trigger: the cue is an intention whose moment the scene reached, a party appearing or the time reaching its due day, and both are of the same kind. This extends ruling 39: a floor is for a cue the scene gave, or for an intention whose trigger the scene's party or time reached. Both join the helper's fixed kind list among the given kinds, take spare turns at root selection as the other given kinds do, and hold their floor at the section caps. Neither is a time kind in the time plan's sense: a due obligation is a candidate only on and after its day and only until the character settles it, so the set is the character's unsettled business and not a calendar readout. Both floors ship at the value the other given kinds have and are named provisional.
- Every admitted open loop or commitment reports, in the result and with the trace off, its direction (owed by the character when the self is among the actors, owed to the character when among the counterparts, or none when the character is not a party or no roles were given) and its due state (overdue, due, not due, or none when no due instant was given), computed against the scene's reference time and its local day. Nothing is removed, omitted or reordered because of either (ADR-D-0018).
- A retrieval on a store holding no roles and no due instants selects what it selects today: the same ids, sections, order and scores, apart from the constructor now naming the self.
- Retrieval reads no clock: the reference scene is captured once, and the same store, scene and input run twice, on any two days, give the same result. This holds for every task and is not restated per task.

## Planner-added requirements
- Direction lives in the assertion vocabulary, not in new fields. Needed because: an assertion already must name one of the memory's subjects (ADR-D-0035's invariant, enforced by the existing validation), which is exactly the rule that a party can never be authored inconsistently with what the memory is about; the assertion node already has graph storage, hydration, replay equality, and a selector pattern by predicate and subject; and ADR-D-0035 admits a new predicate only with a concrete reading behavior, which the trigger route and the direction report are. Two unit predicates, actor and counterpart, each on any number of subjects but never both on one, rather than one predicate with a payload, so the selector that asks "is this notion a party" is one pattern over the subject the assertion already stores and needs no new graph property, and the trigger fires for any party present.
- The trigger route skips the self by the identity the application supplied, and this is the identity's only recall effect. Needed because: the decider ruled that a pair cue needs a counterpart other than the self, and D7 says an intention surfaces when its moment arrives, a person appearing; the character is present at every moment of its own life, so its presence never arrives. Listing the self as present would otherwise contribute every own obligation at the trigger floor on every scene that names it (ruling 12). This is knowing the self by the identity the application gave, which ADR-D-0020's rejected-alternative text allows, and it removes standing rather than granting any; the self listed as a present participant is still treated as any notion by every other road, so a person's own undated promises still surface by topic, by place, by their due instant, or by the self as participant through the state route.
- The identity is checked against the store. Needed because: ADR-D-0020 says a scope may name the same self again and never a different one, and without a check a store opened under another identity reports every direction inverted with nothing to show it. The smallest form: one literal in the store's metadata written on first open, compared at every open, a mismatch refused with one plain error; not a notion, so the self is still never required to exist in the store.
- Due is judged by the scene's local day, not by the instant alone. Needed because: a person wakes carrying what is due today (D1), and a promise due this evening is carried from the morning; the time plan already derives the scene's local day from the offset the application gave, so the boundary is one instant computed in Rust and bound into the query, with no offset handling in the query engine. Due means within the scene's local day; overdue means before it; not due means after it.
- What comes first among many due or triggered obligations is the existing state order, salience then recency of creation then id. Needed because: no number has been measured for "most overdue first", and the state route's order is already what a scope's members follow; a person carries the debts that matter most, and the due state is reported so the renderer can present the oldest if it wants.
- The trigger and due routes contribute obligations only; what an obligation brings is its existing expansion. Needed because: an obligation root of full standing expands as a place-state root does today, so the counterpart's beliefs and the source experience come through the existing links within the existing bounds; that is the one bounded re-cue ADR-D-0023 describes, and no second hop is added.
- The pair counterpart is not a construct. Needed because: in a first-person store, every obligation with one other party is between the character and that party by construction, the assertions name the party and the direction says which way, and the trigger route already fires for the other party present; a pair object would restate what the result already says.

## Scope / Non-goals
- Scope: the production modules, named test files and documents each task lists under owns, no decision record (the two predicates are admitted by ADR-D-0035's rule for new predicates; the identity at construction is ADR-D-0020's own Decision text), this plan.
- Non-goals: a single Preference subtype replacing the user and assistant preference subtypes (the v0.2 draft's deferral note assigned it beside direction, and the state plan's non-goals sent it here; no scenario in the phase's list reads it, and the subject list already says whose preference a memory is once the self is a notion, so it is a rename that changes no behavior and goes to the v0.2 closeout value audit with the user and assistant entity types it belongs beside). Silence relative to a pair's cadence (needs a history of the pair's rhythm; deferred with rhythms). The renderer. Write-path warnings, including the warning that a resolver shares no subject with what it resolves (ruling 42, a separate small plan). Consolidation. A topic trigger beyond the existing content route: an intention whose text matches the topic already comes as a topic hit, and an exact stored trigger phrase would make a description an identity. A resolve method on the facade (the draft admits one only if the scenarios show callers get the recipe wrong; none has). A lookahead ("due in three days") or a snooze. Recurring due instants. A local date field on the obligation (an all-day due is given as the start of that local day, as an instant). A stored pair scope or pair object (ADR-D-0024's pair scope is delivered as direction plus parties; Open Questions). A due instant on episodes, observations or threads. Migrating stored data. Parsing dates or names out of text anywhere.

## Design
- Chosen, the stored shape: two unit predicates in the closed assertion vocabulary, actor and counterpart, each carried by an assertion on one of the memory's notion subjects, and one optional due instant on the interpreted memory, all valid only on an open loop or a commitment. Structure: the domain enum gains two variants and the domain validation gains the subtype rule and the rule that no subject carries both roles; the graph mapping stores the predicate literal on the existing assertion node exactly as known-as is stored, and the due instant as one literal in the stored scene-time form so the time plan's typed cast orders it; hydration reads both back; the drafts and the replacement draft carry the due instant and already carry assertions; replay equality holds because both are fields of the persisted object. Evolution: v0.3 writes these from reflection; v0.4's validity intervals may generalize the due instant. Verification: service-free on the embedded stores through the public facade, each task showing its case failing at its parent commit. Operation: one bounded read per distinct resolved non-self notion for the trigger route plus one read for due, no model call, no clock. Human: the application says who owes whom and by when, as the character's own commitment about the matter, never a reported claim (ADR-D-0035). Safety: nothing here withholds; both roads only add candidates, and the identity of the self removes one standing and grants none.
- Alternative: two fields on the interpreted memory, actor and counterpart. Rejected: it duplicates the subject-consistency rule the assertion vocabulary already enforces, adds two fields across the domain, two drafts, the mapping and hydration, and needs a new graph property for the party where the assertion subject already is one.
- Alternative: one predicate with a payload, owes-to, on the actor. Rejected: the party the trigger route looks for is then sometimes the subject and sometimes the payload, which needs a new graph property and a union in the selector; two unit predicates put every party on the subject the assertion already stores.
- Alternative: parties as memory links between the obligation and the notions. Rejected by ADR-D-0035: a link carries no evidence and cannot be superseded, and a link between an obligation and a person is not an assertion the character makes about that person.
- Chosen, the self: a required constructor argument, the notion id of the character, held in the composition and handed to the retrieval pipeline. At open, before any mutable vector initialization, the graph store's metadata is read for the identity literal: absent, it is written; equal, nothing happens; different, construction fails with one plain error naming both ids, which prevents a silently inverted I-owe-you and you-owe-me. Read in recall exactly once: the trigger route runs for every scene-resolved notion that is not it. Read in reporting exactly once: the direction is the self among the actors or among the counterparts. Must not: be a cue, a root, a floor holder, a filter, a scene participant added by the library, a notion required to exist, or a score term; no selector, no expansion rule and no section rule consults it. The self given as a scene participant by the application is treated by every road as any notion, which is today's behavior.
- Alternative: the self from configuration. Rejected: it is an identity, not a setting, and a setting invites a default the record leaves uncovered.
- Alternative: the self optional, with direction reported as none when absent. Rejected: ADR-D-0020 says the application supplies it at construction, and an optional identity makes every reader carry a case nothing needs; the change to the constructors is mechanical and there are no external consumers.
- Alternative: no identity check against the store, trusting the application as with the collection name. Rejected by Tier A: the failure it leaves is a silently inverted direction on every obligation, and the check is one literal and one read.
- Alternative: the self stored as a notion the store must hold. Rejected: ADR-D-0020 makes the self an ordinary notion the application writes when it first appears, and requiring it at open would make an empty store unopenable.
- Chosen, the trigger route: one read per scene-resolved notion other than the self, through the state selector with the assertion pattern as its scope predicate (the memory carries an assertion whose predicate is actor or counterpart and whose subject is the notion), so the selector's lifecycle and resolution filtering, its salience order and its omission reporting are reused unchanged. The results are explicit roots with score one, full standing, the cue kind trigger, registered in a state scope of the trigger kind per triggering notion, so the existing rounds serve each person's obligations in turn. Score (ruling on the Tier D review): an obligation whose trigger the scene reached is an explicit root with cue score one and full standing, exactly like a keyed participant root, so when the participant road or the topic also reached it the existing union takes the maximum and its final score rises; that is intended, knowing at full strength. Measured at the pinned formulas with salience 0.5: a belief reached from its participant scores 0.6625 at the parent and 0.95 as a triggered root; an obligation the topic hits at 0.2 scores 0.43 at the parent and 0.95 as a triggered root. What rests on such a root also comes closer with it, consistent with ruling 47 (standing follows the road; a full-standing root expands at its own strength): a source episode reached at the parent through participant, obligation, episode at proximity two is reached at proximity one from the obligation root, so with inherited cue 0.75 and salience 0.5 its graph component goes from one third to one half and its final score from 0.6208 to 0.6625; a weak topic hit reached through the obligation can likewise gain inherited cue. Parity is therefore precise: scores are unchanged for memories not reached through an obligation the trigger or due route contributed, as long as the root room was not exhausted; a memory reached through a contributed obligation rises; a memory that held a root slot the obligation takes is afterwards reached only as a descendant, so its score falls, which is the ordinary competition for room every given cue makes and which the report lists with the before and after scores. Such a root keeps the union of its kinds and takes one slot. A superseded obligation is excluded from the trigger and due routes by default and included, after current ones and marked as elsewhere, when the caller includes superseded, since the selector forwards the caller's lifecycle policy. The root source names the trigger for the trace (one added value if the existing sources cannot carry a derived-memory root without changing what they mean today). A description never triggers; a name that could mean two people triggers for both, and the result already says which.
- Alternative: leave the trigger to the state route, which already brings current beliefs about a present notion by salience up to the existing maximum, obligations among them. Rejected unless the baseline shows otherwise: an obligation of low salience about a person with many salient beliefs is cut by that maximum, and the scenarios read the trigger kind; Task_2 shows the failing case first and shrinks to naming the kind if the maximum never cuts it.
- Alternative: the trigger fires only for obligations whose parties are exactly the self and the present notion (the pair reading). Rejected: a person seeing Bob carries what concerns Bob, including a matter between Bob and a third party the character is watching; the direction report says the character is not a party, and the renderer decides what to voice.
- Chosen, the due route: one read per retrieval through the same state selector, with a scope predicate that binds the due literal and compares it, by the typed cast, to the first instant of the day after the scene's local day, computed in Rust from the scene's offset; results are explicit roots with score one, full standing and the cue kind due. Resolved and fulfilled obligations are filtered by the selector with the existing resolution reason. Due and trigger both join the helper's fixed kind list between activity and date match, hold their floors from their own orders, take spare turns at root selection, and fill spare room at the other stages by the existing score order, exactly as place does.
- Alternative: due judged by the instant alone (at or before the reference time). Rejected: a promise due at five is carried all day, and the scene's local day already exists.
- Chosen, the due floor's justification: ADR-D-0022 names the stored intention's trigger as a cue kind; a due instant is an intention whose moment the time reached, the same kind of cue as a party appearing, so it holds a floor as any given cue does. This is an extension of ruling 39, logged in the Decision Log: a floor is for a cue the scene gave, or for an intention whose trigger the scene's time or party reached. An all-day due is given as the start of that local day, as an instant; no local date field exists on the obligation.
- Alternative: due as a time kind with a zero floor and no spare turns, like recency. Rejected: recency has a candidate on every scene forever, which is why its floor is zero; a due obligation is an intention whose trigger the time reached, a candidate only on and after its day and only until the character settles it, and D8 says it comes whatever the topic, which only a floor guarantees under a loud topic.
- Alternative: a window after which an overdue obligation stops coming. Rejected: a number nobody measured, and the remedy for a stale debt is resolution or supersession, which are recorded decisions (ADR-D-0018).
- Chosen, the report: two optional values on the existing included-derived-memory entry beside resolved-by, computed at pack assembly from the assertions, the self and the scene's reference time; no second store read.
- Alternative: report the self's id once on the result and let readers compare. Rejected: the scenarios and the renderer read direction per obligation, and the parties are already visible in the assertions, so the enum is the only thing the reader cannot compute without the identity.
- Measurement (standing rule of 2026-09-22): every task that changes what comes to mind, its order or its standing carries a measurement validation owned by the evals side, run at the task's tip before its pull request opens, before and after on the same instrument and inputs, in both id orders, twice. Instrument: the companion evaluation repository's Task_9 generated runner (the M3 pattern) extended with an obligations family: a keyed person with obligations in both directions, some resolved by a later memory, some with due instants before and some after the reference time, one own promise with no party, beside unrelated topic pressure that fills every cap, with ids opposed to time and salience. Falsifiers, any one of which falsifies the design: an obligation to the person present does not surface under a loud topic; a resolved obligation surfaces as open (not marked resolved-by, or contributed by trigger or due); an own promise with no party and no due instant surfaces on every retrieval; on-topic memories kept drop below the topic-alone level when obligations are present beyond the two floors' reservations. Task_1 measures the parent-equality control and the resolved-as-open falsifier on the report; Task_2 measures the person-present and no-party falsifiers and the on-topic level; Task_3 measures the due falsifiers, the resolved-as-open falsifier on the due road and the on-topic level. The validator's vocabulary has no measurement kind, so each item is carried as a manual validation owned by the orchestrator, whose detail names the evals worker as the runner.
- Why chosen: the smallest shape that lets an obligation say who owes whom and by when, reuses the state selector, its resolution filter, the explicit-root path and the floors helper, and gives the self one recall effect and one reporting effect, the readers the rulings deferred to this plan. Fit: the phase draft sections 2 and 2.2; philosophy "what is owed in either direction, what fell due today" and "purpose surfaces from memory"; ADR-D-0018, D-0020, D-0022, D-0023, D-0029, D-0035, D-0038; rulings 12, 17, 20, 23, 26, 27, 39, 42, 45, 46, 47.
