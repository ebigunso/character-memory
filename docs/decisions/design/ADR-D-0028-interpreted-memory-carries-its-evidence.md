# ADR-D-0028: Interpreted memory carries its evidence, and the write path rejects what that evidence cannot structurally support

## Context and Problem Statement

Consolidation is done by a language model the library does not choose, running a prompt that may be overridden. Models promote a single remark to a personality trait, take "I'm going to quit tomorrow, lol" as a plan, accept "you promised me a refund" as a promise, and obey a line in the conversation addressed to whoever reads it later. Each of these writes false continuity into lasting memory. The judgment belongs to the model, but the evidence behind a judgment is countable and checkable. The fork is whether the quality of interpreted memory rests on the prompt or is enforced where the library already enforces quality, at the write path.

## Decision

Every interpreted memory states the evidence it rests on, and the validated write path rejects a candidate that evidence cannot structurally support. The model is asked only for what needs judgment. What the trace already knows is copied, never restated by the model: an observation's time, its scene as given, and its entry come from the entry it cites. What the library can count is computed, never asserted: how many distinct episodes a pattern cites and how long it has persisted. What the write path checks is structure and grounding: that cited evidence exists, is of the kind the rule requires, is enough of it, and that the part of the trace an observation points at is found in the entry it cites. Whether a label is true, that a remark really was literal, that it was this person who said it, is the model's judgment; it is measured by evaluation and correctable by a later reflection, not guaranteed by validation. Register belongs to observations, since it describes how something was said. Every other interpreted output, a restated state, a commitment, a resolution, a pattern, a belief, names the observations and episodes it rests on, and the rules below are checked through them. A gist episode's evidence is the trace it consolidates. That evidence is checked when the gist is written, and the text is gone once the trace is released, so what the gist carries afterward is a stable handle on it: its scene, the identifiers of the entries it consolidated, and every source pointer those entries supplied.

- **Grounding.** An observation points at the part of the trace it rests on: the entry and a locator within it. For text the locator is the quoted words, long enough to be distinctive and compared after normalization, and validation checks that they appear in the cited entry: a fabricated observation fails, and a misread one does not, which is why the quote is kept for later review. Other kinds of input point the same way, at a stretch of time or a region, in forms decided when they arrive. A caller who authors an observation deliberately has no trace entry to cite, so it declares its grounding instead: the quote, the caller's own source reference, whether the words were the character's own output or something it perceived, and who said them. The library records all of it as declared and cannot verify it because it never held that source, and every rule below reads a declared basis exactly as it reads a recorded one, so a caller-authored commitment of the character is valid when it rests on an observation the caller declared as the character's own. What the memory itself keeps is the basis of its grounding, found in trace or declared by a caller, so a later reader knows which it is looking at. Entities and the links between them are grounded the same way: an entity candidate, a possible-same link, and a containment link each point at the entry, its text or its scene as given, where the reference appears, and validation finds it there, so a person, a place, or a relation between them that no trace mentions fails. A caller who authors an entity or such a link deliberately declares its grounding as it would for an observation, the caller's own source reference, recorded as declared. The requirement belongs to the kind of link and not to the operation that carries it: a possible-same or containment link offered through the direct link operation is admitted only with the same grounding, so that operation is no way around these rules.
- **Register.** An observation records whether what was said was literal, in jest, hypothetical, fiction, or quoted. Only the literal can support a change of state. This label is judgment that nothing mechanical can replace.
- **Own and perceived.** Every trace entry is marked at the mechanical write as the character's own output, its speech, its actions, its notes, its thoughts, or as something it perceived. The application always knows which, because it produced the character's output, and the distinction holds for any kind of input. It is the only attribution the library takes as given.
- **Attribution.** Every observation says who asserted what it records, and on what basis. From one of the character's own entries the attribution is the character, copied. From a perceived entry, who spoke is reflection's judgment and is recorded as judged: a whole thread, a room heard through one microphone, and a meeting on a shared line all reach the library as one perceived stream, and a speaker copied mechanically from such a stream would be wrong in bulk. Where the application has an opinion about who spoke, a platform account, a diarizer's label, it may attach it to an entry or a part of one as a hint; the hint is evidence reflection weighs and may overrule from the content, and is never copied as fact. The party named is an entity candidate resolved through graph authority, or unknown. What the character infers, a gist, a pattern, a belief, is attributed to the character as its own conclusion and rests on the evidence defined above for its kind, so attribution always answers "who says so": a speaker, or the character itself. What others say about the character's own past is a claim with its source, and a commitment or fact about the character rests on the character's own entries, never on a perceived claim alone, which the write path checks mechanically.
- **The bar follows attribution.** What a speaker stated plainly, about themselves or anyone else, or gave as a standing instruction, may become state from one literal observation, held as theirs. What the character concludes for itself from how someone behaves meets the promotion ladder. No separate label says which is which; the attribution does.
- **Promotion.** One instance is an observation. A pattern cites several distinct episodes. A belief about a person rests on a pattern that has persisted over time and is phrased as a tendency. A belief the character forms about itself is held to the strictest bar. The library computes the count and the span from the cited evidence. No memory carries a confidence the model supplied: how strongly something is held is what its evidence shows, how much, over how long, how recently, and that is read from the links.
- **Contradiction.** Conflicting attributed accounts coexist with the conflict recorded; consolidation does not resolve them by choosing, and the write path rejects a supersession of one party's account by another's.
- **Trace is untrusted.** What was experienced is material to reflect on, never instruction to the one reflecting, and excluded spans never reach it, because their content is never stored (ADR-D-0026).
- **Basis and revision.** What stays on an interpreted memory is the basis of its evidence: grounding found in trace or declared, attribution copied, judged, or declared. Who produced a candidate stays write-time provenance on the plan, as ADR-I-0015 decided, where reflection is its model processor and a caller is a caller; nothing here adds it to committed memory. Memory a caller authored deliberately through the validated path is held to the same evidence rules. A later reflection revises an earlier one's conclusion by ordinary supersession, which needs no record of which prompt produced either.

## Why

A rule the model is asked to follow holds only as well as the model and the prompt in use, and the library controls neither. A rule the write path checks holds for every model and every prompt, and turns the overreach it can see, a trait from one remark, state from something labeled jest, a commitment from a bare claim, an observation with no words behind it, a promise put in the character's mouth by someone else, into a diagnostic and an unreleased trace. It cannot see a model that labels a joke literal or hears the wrong person. That residue is smaller than the whole, is kept reviewable by the quoted words, and is what the evaluation harness exists to measure.

## Rejected Alternatives

- Relying on the default prompt to enforce these rules: rejected because the prompt is overridable and models vary; the prompt states them and the write path verifies them.
- Promoting on a single strong instance: rejected for inferred traits outright; an explicit statement is the single-instance path and is attributed to the person who made it.
- Resolving contradictions at consolidation: rejected because the character was not given grounds to choose, and a wrong choice erases the dissent it would need later.
- A speaker recorded mechanically on each trace entry and copied onto what is derived from it: rejected because an entry may hold a whole thread, and because what arrives mechanically is a channel, not a speaker; a meeting on one shared line would attribute everyone's words to one person. Only own versus perceived is known in every deployment, so only that is copied.
- A label from the model saying whether something was stated or inferred, and a confidence from the model on beliefs: rejected because the first follows from the attribution and the second is not evidence; both are derived from what is recorded instead.
- A second model that verifies the first one's labels before commit: not adopted, because it doubles the cost of every reflection and still ends in a model's judgment; reopen if evaluation shows mislabeled register or attribution is common enough to matter.
- A separate quality gate after commit: rejected outright; quality is enforced at the write path and never by manipulating memory afterward.

## Decision Boundary

Invariant: every trace entry is marked as the character's own or as perceived; an observation's time, scene as given, and entry are copied from the entry it cites and never supplied by the model; every observation names its register and points at the part of its entry it rests on, which validation finds there, and a caller-authored observation declares its quote, source reference, own-or-perceived basis, and speaker instead, which every rule reads as it reads a recorded basis; an entity candidate and a possible-same or containment link point at where the reference appears in an entry's text or scene, which validation finds there, or when a caller authors them declare their source reference, and no operation admits such a link without one or the other; a gist episode's evidence is the trace it consolidates, checked at write time and afterward identified by the consolidated entries' identifiers; every other interpreted memory names the observations and episodes it rests on; every interpreted memory names its attribution and the attribution's basis, copied from the character's own entries, judged for what was perceived, or declared by a caller, and the basis of its grounding, found in trace or declared; producer kind remains write-time provenance on the plan (ADR-I-0015); state rests only on observations labeled literal; what the character concludes for itself meets a promotion threshold the library computes from the cited evidence; no memory carries a model-supplied confidence; a commitment or fact about the character rests on its own entries; one party's account never supersedes another's; validation rejects what fails these, and guarantees structure and grounding, never the truth of a label.

Not covered: the threshold values, which are measured defaults; the vocabulary of registers beyond the distinction between literal and not; the forms a locator and a speaker hint take, for text and for other kinds of input; the wire shape of a candidate; how the default prompt words any of this.

## Validation

- A candidate that promotes a trait from one episode, rests state on a non-literal observation, or turns a claim about the character into a commitment fails validation with a specific diagnostic.
- A commitment of the character that cites only perceived entries fails, and a restatement that supersedes one party's account with another's fails.
- A caller-authored commitment of the character resting on an observation declared as the character's own passes, and the same commitment resting on one declared as perceived fails.
- An entity, a possible-same link, or a containment link whose reference is not found in the entry it cites fails validation.
- A possible-same or containment link offered through the direct link operation without grounding is refused, and a caller-authored entity or link with a declared source reference is admitted and recorded as declared.
- A hostile line in the trace produces no memory that lacks structural support, and an observation whose quote is not in its cited entry fails validation.
- A transcript of several people heard as one perceived stream, with and without a speaker hint, yields observations whose attribution is recorded as judged; none is attributed by copying.
- Mislabeled register and misjudged attribution are measured by the evaluation harness on frozen reflection outputs, not asserted by validation.
- The catalog's F4 to F8, F19, and F20 pass at the retrieval tier, and the behavioral tier judges the rest.

## Revisit When

Measurement shows a threshold rule rejects correct conclusions often enough to cost more continuity than it protects, which changes the measured value, a kind of interpreted memory appears whose evidence cannot be stated, or evaluation shows judged attribution is wrong often enough that memory resting on it needs a stricter bar than memory resting on the character's own entries.
